Tricloud, a Danish Industrial IoT company, joined the Margo open source initiative as a contributor to help develop open standards for device orchestration. Tricloud's CTO Mikael Nygaard will join the technical working group and its Nexus IIoT platform aligns with Margo's use of OPC-UA, MQTT, OpenTelemetry, and Kubernetes.
