The industrial edge computing market is projected to grow from $21.19 billion in 2025 to $44.73 billion by 2030, yet remains fragmented and proprietary. Margo's Preview Release 1 offers the first open-source sandbox with fleet management software, application, and virtualized device components, letting adopters test interoperability principles ahead of the 2026 general release.
