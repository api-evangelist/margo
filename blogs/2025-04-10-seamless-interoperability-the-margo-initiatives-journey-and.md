A recap of a Hannover Messe 2025 panel discussion reflecting on Margo's first year, featuring Bart Nieuwborg (Chair), Carl Moberg (Avassa), Ashwani Singh (Belden), and Michael Maxey (Zededa). Panelists discussed community growth, real-world multi-vendor interoperability challenges, and Margo's reliance on Kubernetes, Docker, and Podman for edge container orchestration.
