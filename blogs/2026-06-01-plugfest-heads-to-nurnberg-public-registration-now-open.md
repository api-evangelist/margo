Margo opens registration for its Plugfest, taking place July 7-10, 2026 at SUSE's offices in Nurnberg, Germany, following the momentum of Preview Release 1 (PR1). The event offers collaborative testing of application definitions, fleet management strategies, and device requirements, plus direct engagement with the Technical Working Group.
