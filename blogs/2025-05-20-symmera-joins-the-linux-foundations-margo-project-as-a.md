Symmera, an XIoT and AI/ML enablement company, joined the Margo alliance as a contributor to help define an open standards-based solution for IoT/OT connected devices. CEO Srinivas Kumar joins the technical working group, contributing Symmera's patented Distributed Intelligent Network platform expertise for device trust and orchestration.
